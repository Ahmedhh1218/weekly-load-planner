Privacy

Weekly Load Planner stores everything you enter on your own device, in your browser's storage. There is no account and no server that receives your plans, income or expenses.

What stays on your device

Your plans, blocks, categories, income sources, expenses and settings. Nobody else can read them, including the person who made the app. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Backup to keep a copy.

What the app sends out

When you are online, the app asks a public exchange rate service (ExchangeRate-API, with a fallback to a public currency data file on the jsDelivr network) for current rates. The request names your main currency and nothing you have entered. Like any web request, it exposes your IP address to that service. You can turn this off by entering your own rates in the money settings and staying offline.

What it does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts. Fonts are served with the app.
