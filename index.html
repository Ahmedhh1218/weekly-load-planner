Drag blocks. See the load.

Move, stretch and add blocks on a week timeline. The charts update as you go: hours per category, and each day measured against what it can really hold once meals and commuting are taken out.

Switch from hours to effort when a two-hour exam prep drains you more than a two-hour walk. You give each category its own weight.

Money sits next to the schedule.

Add income as a fixed monthly amount or an hourly rate, in any currency. Hourly income reads its hours from your schedule, so a busier week shows up in the month.

Log expenses day by day and see the month at a glance: what came in, what went out, what you kept. Exchange rates update by themselves when you are online.

Your plans stay on your device.

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No ads and no analytics.
  • Backup saves everything to a file. Restore loads it on any device, so that is how you move from your laptop to your phone.

The only thing the app sends out is a request for exchange rates. It names a currency and nothing you have entered. Read the privacy note.

Install it in a few taps.

Once installed it opens full screen, has its own icon and works without a connection.

iPhone and iPadSafari
  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

You are not in Safari. Open this page in Safari to install.

  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Updates arrive by themselves.

When a new version is ready, the app shows a banner. Tap Reload to update. Your plans and money data stay where they are.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The code is open source under the MIT licence, and you can read it on GitHub.

Do my plans sync between my phone and laptop?

Not automatically. Use Backup on one device and Restore on the other. Automatic sync would need accounts and a server, and that is a bigger trade-off for your privacy.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Plans live in the browser's storage, so clearing site data deletes them. Safari can also clear storage for sites you rarely open. Press Backup now and then to keep a copy.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From a free public rates service, fetched when you are online. If you prefer, type your own rate for any currency in the money settings.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only for exchange rates. Everything else works offline once the app has loaded.

Which browsers work?

Current Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Installing as an app works in Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome and Edge on Android and desktop.
